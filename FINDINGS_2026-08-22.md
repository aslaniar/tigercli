## 15.12 HELMET BUG: THE CONTENDER ROUND-UP (2026-08-22 ~15:0x) - no deep dive yet

USER CORRECTION (important, reframes the target): this may be stock SUNRISE

### TWO FACTS GATHERED FOR THIS ROUND-UP

- FAMILY-0 vs FAMILY-3 DIVERGENCE IS ESSENTIALLY REFUTED. Both records are
  built by the SAME build_shared() and carry the byte-identical Appearance
  block (character_record_encoder.cpp:101-131); only the tails differ (family-3
  gets kPreviewFlagOffsets, family-0 gets a 24-byte tail). The preview cannot
  be seeing a different appearance block. Demote to a long shot on the tails.
- WE PUBLISH SEVERAL DIRECTLY OBSERVABLE PREFERENCES alongside helmetMode:
  showFps, brightness, subtitlesMode, colorblindMode, hudOpacity,
  reticleLocation, reticleColor, textSize (preferences_encoder.cpp:88-103).
  That makes contender 1 testable for free.

### THE CONTENDERS, RANKED BY (LIKELIHOOD x CHEAPNESS)

**1. THE WHOLE PREFERENCES RECORD IS INERT** - the client never applies what we
   publish, and the preview falls back to a default of helmet-off.
   FITS THE SPLIT: the in-world renderer may not consult the helmet preference
   at all in this flow, so it draws everything; the preview does consult it,
   gets nothing, and takes its fallback.
   SUPPORTING EVIDENCE: flipping helmet_mode 1->0 changed nothing (15.10) -
   exactly what "the record is never read" predicts.
   DISCRIMINATOR (FREE, no inventory involved, no graphics delta): change an
   OBSERVABLE preference in settings.json and boot. show_fps is the cleanest
   (D2 has its own FPS readout, distinct from the Metal HUD); subtitles_mode,
   hud_opacity, reticle_color and text_size are backups. If NONE of them take
   effect, the entire preferences record is inert and helmet_mode could never
   have worked - the front becomes "wire up preferences consumption", not a
   helmet problem.
   COST: one boot, one config byte, zero risk.

**2. THE RULE LIVES IN AN UNMAPPED REGION WE PUBLISH AS ZEROS.**
   The fork maps what exploration needed and pads the rest. Preview-adjacent
   unmapped space: kProgressPreviewPaddingSize = **792 bytes sitting directly
   before previewMirrors**, plus kPreviewProgressionPaddingSize = 5. In the
   shared Appearance block: indexBank[48], smallBankA/B/C[16 each], keyedRows[8]
   - all unexplained banks. A per-character appearance/preview toggle could sit
   in any of them, and the 792-byte region's adjacency to the preview mirrors is
   suggestive rather than random.
   FITS THE SPLIT: a preview-only field would by definition not affect in-world.
   DISCRIMINATOR: cheap first pass = confirm what we actually emit there (all
   zeros?) from the wire; then a targeted probe. No rebuild to inspect.
   COST: low to inspect, medium to test.

**3. THE unlockPairs STUB GATES PER-SLOT ART IN THE PREVIEW.**
   2 pairs per render row, {definitionHash, int32 "the value THE ACCOUNT STATE
   SUPPLIES"}, sentinel-filled for every slot and never populated
   (character_appearance_sentinels.cpp:16).
   FITS THE SPLIT: if the preview evaluates per-slot unlock gates and the
   in-world renderer does not, empty pairs could read as "not permitted" ->
   hide. The helmet is the slot most likely to carry a real appearance/ornament
   unlock.
   WEAKNESS: armor renders fine in the preview with the same empty pairs, so it
   would have to be helmet-specific in the CLIENT's data, not ours.
   AND NOTE: flags/unlocks were the answer to the weapons bug - unlock-shaped
   stubs deserve suspicion.
   COST: medium (needs a rebuild to populate).

**4. THE CLIENT'S OWN MISSING-CONTEXT FALLBACK.**
   Direct evidence the client has such fallbacks, from the fork's own comment
   (character_record_encoder.cpp:108-109): "their accessor returns true when the
   context is missing, so a cleared flag asserts the opposite of the client's
   own fallback." If the preview asks "show the helmet?" with a missing context,
   its fallback may be hide.
   COST: HIGH - this is the client-RE deep dive. Do not start it until 1 and 2
   are done, because either could make it unnecessary.

**5. (LONG SHOT) THE FAMILY-3 / FAMILY-0 TAILS.** The shared block is refuted
   above, but the tails are not identical. kPreviewFlagOffsets are written from
   previewAvailable=1. Cheap to re-read if 1-3 all fail.

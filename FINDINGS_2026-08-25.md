### What this settles

**Acknowledgement is content-gated, and the ack path works mid-session.** This
was the pre-named third branch and the one that would have been most expensive
to be wrong about: it was inferred from earlier boots that the client acks our
solo body, and that inference had been promoted to fact without ever being
tested under the current build. It now holds by execution. 20.48's reading
stands - the peer ROW is refused; the acknowledgement is not broken.

### The instrument bug that cost the other four shapes

`sweep_variant` anchored its phase to the first membership body of the BOOT
(t~325466, the Mac signing in alone), not to the first peer-bearing one. By the
time the rig joined at t=450034 the clock had already advanced
(450034 - 325466) / 30000 = 4.15 steps, so the sweep OPENED on index 4,
`full_mirror`, stepped to `solo`, and terminated. `key_only`, `key_account`,
`key_account_join` and `key_account_join_opaque` never shipped.

The 27-second peer window was not the user quitting and not a short-lived peer
condition - it was the sweep landing on its own terminal control almost
immediately.

Fixed in p2(40) with three corrections: anchor to the first PEER-BEARING body;
advance on a body COUNT rather than a wall clock, so a shape is measured by how
many transmissions carried it; and keep a time floor as well, because bodies
arrive in bursts (three inside one second at t=450034/450099/450919) and a pure
count would skip shapes the client never saw settle.

### Still open

Which field, or whether any second row is structurally acceptable at all. The
next run opens on `key_only` and walks. Pre-named: a peer variant acking names
the field; reaching `solo` again means no peer-bearing shape is acceptable and
the Ghidra lane should target table-level validation rather than field
semantics; `full_mirror` appearing as the FIRST variant line means the
anchoring is still wrong and the run is void.
